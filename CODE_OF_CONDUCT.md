# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in LoomCheck respectful, constructive, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, experience, identity, or communication style.

## Expected behavior

Use welcoming language, assume good intent while addressing impact, provide actionable technical feedback, and accept correction gracefully. Keep discussions focused on the problem and its evidence rather than on people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized language, threats, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or edit contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
